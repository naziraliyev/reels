SQL quiz: hech qator topilmasa SUM nima qaytaradi — 0 mi yoki NULL? 🤔
Javob: NULL! COUNT(*) esa 0 qaytaradi.
✅ SUM, AVG, MIN, MAX — qator bo'lmasa NULL
✅ COUNT(*) va COUNT(ustun) — har doim son, bo'sh bo'lsa 0
✅ Hisobotda 0 kerak bo'lsa: COALESCE(SUM(summa), 0)
