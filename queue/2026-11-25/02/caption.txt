Seriya: SQL o'rta daraja #1 — Window funksiyalar (suhbatlarda eng ko'p so'raladigan mavzu)
✅ OVER (PARTITION BY bolim ORDER BY maosh DESC) — qatorlar guruhlanmaydi, har qator yoniga o'z guruhidagi o'rni yoziladi
✅ Teng maoshlar (1200, 1200, 900): ROW_NUMBER → 1, 2, 3 · RANK → 1, 1, 3 · DENSE_RANK → 1, 1, 2
✅ Top-1 har guruhda: WITH r AS (... RANK() OVER (...) AS o ...) SELECT ... FROM r WHERE o = 1
⚠️ WHERE ROW_NUMBER() OVER (...) = 1 → xato: window funksiya WHERE'dan KEYIN hisoblanadi. CTE yoki subquery'ga o'rang.
